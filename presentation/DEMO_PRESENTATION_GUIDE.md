### Step 3: Live Interactive Q&A Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: Finding Mandatory Document Uploads
- **User Action**: Click the quick prompt button `"Where do I upload my documents?"` (or type it into the input box).
- **AI Behavior**:
  - Gemma 3 (via local Ollama) analyzes the page context extract.
  - Returns: *"You can upload your documents in the 'Document Upload Section' located near the bottom of the form."*
  - **Magic Moment**: Automatically highlights `#btn-upload-docs` and the document dropzone with a glowing cyan outline and smooth scroll into view!

#### Scenario 2: Understanding Mandatory Fields
- **User Action**: Click `"Which fields are required?"`.
- **AI Behavior**:
  - Responds with required inputs: *Applicant Full Name, Aadhaar Number, Annual Family Income, and Income Certificate*.
  - Highlights input fields `#full-name`, `#aadhaar-number`, and `#annual-income`.

#### Scenario 3: Page Overview & Policy Simplification
- **User Action**: Click `"What is this page about?"` or type *"Explain eligibility policy"*.
- **AI Behavior**:
  - Explains the scholarship portal scope and income ceiling criteria in plain, jargon-free language.
