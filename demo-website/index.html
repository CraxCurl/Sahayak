❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for full tuition waiver?

Students with annual family income less than ₹2,500,000 enrolled in accredited technical or skill programs.

Is Aadhaar linkage mandatory?

Yes, Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) requires your bank account to be linked and seeded with your Aadhaar number.
